## Fixed

- **Astro idles on much less power.** A page that offers passkey sign-in, like Google's, kept Chrome's Bluetooth code looking for a nearby phone the whole time the tab was open, checking twice a second. Now it only looks while you're actually signing in with a phone. With eleven tabs open and Astro hidden, it draws about a third of what it did.
- **Links open faster.** A link from another app shows its page in Little Astro about 100 ms sooner, and the page takes no clicks or typing until the address bar shows where it is.
